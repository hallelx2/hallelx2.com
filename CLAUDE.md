# hallelx2.com — Labs site

The public hallelx2 labs site. Design is ported, not authored, here: the v10
static HTML set in OpenDesign project `240c474c-aa3f-4988-be63-7820b43deb83`
is the source of truth, `docs/DESIGN.md` is the contract, and
`scripts/port.mjs` regenerates every generated file. Do not hand-edit files
carrying a "generated by scripts/port.mjs" header.

Hard rules carried from the design contract:

- Only `src/styles/tokens.css` declares `:root` or `@font-face`.
- No gradients, blurs, or blend modes. No dark theme. No invented metrics.
- Retail faces (Haffer, Brisa Pro) stay out of the repo until licensed (HAL-1393).
- Internal links stay plain `<a>` (deliberate MPA — see README).
- Verify visually by pixel-diff against the OpenDesign originals under
  `--force-prefers-reduced-motion`, at 390 / 1100 / 1440.

Deploy: Vercel, production domain `hallelx2.com` (DNS at Hostinger).
